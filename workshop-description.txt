[h1]贸易飞船（续） / Trader Ships (Continued)[/h1]

飞船会降落到你的地图上，和你当面交易——不需要轨道商船，也不需要贸易信标。

这是 [b]AUTOMATIC1111[/b] 原作 [i]Trader ships[/i] 的 [b]1.7[/b] 续作，由 IoIqq 移植并扩展。

[hr][/hr]

[h1]这一版新增[/h1]

[h2]贸易台[/h2]
独立的 3×2 供电建筑，与通讯台同科技、造价更低。小人右键即可呼叫或购买飞船，不必再绕通讯台。

[h2]派系专属船队[/h2]
每个派系会按自己的物品库自动生成 [b]9 类主题商船[/b]：

[list]
[*][b]医疗船[/b] —— 药品与医疗物资
[*][b]武器船[/b] —— 武器、弹药，夹带医疗品
[*][b]服装船[/b] —— 衣物、护甲、头盔
[*][b]食品船[/b] —— 成品餐与饮品
[*][b]物资船[/b] —— 金属、皮革、布料、矿产
[*][b]工业船[/b] —— 零件、燃料、化工
[*][b]家具船[/b] —— 能搬走的家具与建材
[*][b]珍宝船[/b] —— 神器、遗物、超科技小件
[*][b]杂货船[/b] —— 什么都有一点
[/list]

外加两种卖人的船：

[list]
[*][b]人才船[/b] —— 4~8 人，技能 14~20 且带热情，[b]买回来直接是殖民者[/b]
[*][b]奴隶船[/b] —— 10~18 人，便宜量大，买回来是奴隶
[/list]

[h2]船型与货单[/h2]
货单按主题从[b]原版分类与交易标签[/b]里挑选：普通船约 40 种、食品与杂货船约 80 种，不会出现一堆同名变体。

医疗船真的只卖药，武器船不会夹带粮食。任何船都会收购自己经营的那类物品，交易窗口的「会购买的物品」会列出完整分类。

[h2]包船与买船[/h2]
付银呼叫指定船型，好感越高越便宜（好感 +100 时 6 折）。也可以向派系直接[b]买下一艘属于自己的穿梭机[/b]，价格随好感浮动。

[h2]飞船会被打[/h2]
血量掉到 60% 以下，船长会要求赔偿。三种结果：

[list]
[*]付钱了事
[*]拒绝 —— 扣好感，并发信件通知你具体掉了多少
[*]拒绝并承担后果 —— 该派系转为敌对
[/list]

直接击毁飞船，[b]整船货物会散落一地[/b]，派系立刻与你为敌。

[quote]只有[b]你自己的殖民者或炮塔[/b]打伤飞船，船长才会找你赔钱。火灾、雷击、别的派系交火不算在你头上。[/quote]

[h2]偷渡客[/h2]
飞船有几率带下一个[b]无阵营[/b]的人，在殖民地附近徘徊。收留就编入殖民地，拒绝则自行离开。

[hr][/hr]

[h1]用法[/h1]

[list]
[*]研究微电子学，在建筑菜单找到[b]贸易台[/b]（3×2，钢 80 + 零件 2，需要通电）
[*]小人右键贸易台，选[b]Trade[/b]
[*]左侧选派系，右侧选船型，点[b]呼叫[/b]
[*]飞船降落后，小人走到船边交易
[/list]

[h1]着陆区怎么画[/h1]

区域分类里有两个工具：「[b]扩展着陆区[/b]」「[b]清除着陆区[/b]」。

飞船瞄准[b]整片区域的中心[/b]降落——想让好几艘船并排停靠，请划[b]几块互不相连的小区域[/b]，而不是一整块大区域。

[h1]前置[/h1]

[list]
[*]环世界 [b]1.7[/b]（同时保留 1.1~1.6 的旧版本文件夹）
[*][b]Harmony[/b] —— [u]可选[/u]
[/list]

不装 Harmony 会怎样？飞船照常来、照常交易，核心功能不受影响。只有一件事失效：通讯台不会多出「贸易台……」子菜单——用独立的贸易台建筑即可，其余完全一致。

不需要任何 DLC。拥有 Royalty 时飞船还会使用降落信标作为备选落点。

[h1]兼容性[/h1]

[list]
[*]适用版本：1.7（同时声明 1.1~1.6）
[*]会新增 Def（贸易台、自动生成的船型），不改动原版 Def 的数值
[*]可以中途加入现有存档
[*][b]不建议中途移除[/b]——地图上正在交易的飞船会消失，着陆区也会一并丢弃
[*]若装了重度改动交易界面的模组，船型列表可能错位
[/list]

[h1]所以为什么值得装[/h1]

原版的轨道商船只能隔着通讯台买卖。这个模组让飞船[b]真的落到你的地图上[/b]，你得派小人走过去，货是真放在船上的，船被打会掉货、会翻脸、会赔钱——交易第一次有了物理存在。

[h1]Trader Ships (Continued)[/h1]

Trade ships land on your map and do business with you face to face - no orbital trader, no trade beacon.

The RimWorld 1.7 continuation of AUTOMATIC1111's Trader ships, ported and extended by IoIqq.

[hr][/hr]

[h1]New in this version[/h1]

[h2]Trade Desk[/h2]
A standalone powered 3x2 building with the same research as the comms console and a lower cost. Right-click it to call or buy ships without touching the console.

[h2]Faction fleets[/h2]
Every faction automatically gets up to [b]9 themed trade ships[/b] drawn from its own item pool:

[list]
[*][b]Medical[/b] - medicine and medical supplies
[*][b]Weapon[/b] - weapons and ammo, with some medicine
[*][b]Apparel[/b] - clothing, armour, headgear
[*][b]Food[/b] - prepared meals and drinks
[*][b]Supply[/b] - metal, leather, textiles, minerals
[*][b]Industrial[/b] - components, fuel, chemicals
[*][b]Furniture[/b] - furniture and building materials
[*][b]Relic[/b] - artifacts, relics, ultratech
[*][b]General[/b] - a bit of everything
[/list]

Plus two ships that sell people:

[list]
[*][b]Talent[/b] - 4-8 people with skills 14-20 and a passion, who [b]join as colonists[/b]
[*][b]Slave[/b] - 10-18 people, cheaper and in bulk, who arrive as slaves
[/list]

[h2]Stock[/h2]
Stock is drawn from [b]vanilla categories and trade tags[/b] matching the theme: around 40 item kinds on a normal ship, around 80 on food and general ships, with no duplicate variants.

Medical ships really only sell medicine; weapon ships carry no food. Every ship buys back the categories it deals in, and the "will buy" tab lists them in full.

[h2]Charter and purchase[/h2]
Pay silver to call a specific ship type - better goodwill means a lower fee (40% off at +100). You can also [b]buy a shuttle of your own[/b] from a faction, priced by goodwill.

[h2]Ships can be attacked[/h2]
Below 60% health the captain demands compensation. Three outcomes:

[list]
[*]Pay up and it leaves
[*]Refuse - goodwill drops, and a letter tells you exactly how much
[*]Refuse and accept the consequences - the faction turns hostile
[/list]

Destroy the ship outright and [b]the entire cargo spills onto the ground[/b], while the faction becomes an enemy.

[quote]The captain only demands compensation when [b]your own colonists or turrets[/b] did the damage. Fires, lightning and other factions' fights are not charged to you.[/quote]

[h2]Stowaways[/h2]
A ship may drop off a [b]factionless[/b] person who wanders near your colony. Take them in and they join; turn them away and they leave.

[hr][/hr]

[h1]How to use[/h1]

[list]
[*]Research Microelectronics, then find [b]Trade Desk[/b] in the architect menu (3x2, 80 steel + 2 components, needs power)
[*]Right-click the desk with a colonist and pick [b]Trade[/b]
[*]Pick a faction on the left, a ship type on the right, then hit [b]Call[/b]
[*]Once it lands, walk a colonist over and trade
[/list]

[h1]Landing zones[/h1]

Two tools live in the Zone category: [b]Expand landing zone[/b] and [b]Clear landing zone[/b].

Ships aim for the [b]centre of the whole zone[/b] - if you want several ships parked side by side, paint [b]several separate small zones[/b] rather than one big one.

[h1]Requirements[/h1]

[list]
[*]RimWorld [b]1.7[/b] (older 1.1-1.6 folders are still bundled)
[*][b]Harmony[/b] - [u]optional[/u]
[/list]

What happens without Harmony? Ships still arrive and still trade; core functionality is unaffected. Only one thing is lost: the comms console no longer gains a "Trade desk..." submenu - use the standalone building instead and everything else is identical.

No DLC required. With Royalty, ships will also use your ship landing beacons as a fallback landing spot.

[h1]Compatibility[/h1]

[list]
[*]Version: 1.7 (1.1-1.6 also declared)
[*]Adds new Defs (trade desk, generated ship types); does not rewrite vanilla Def values
[*]Safe to add to an existing save
[*][b]Removing it mid-save is not recommended[/b] - ships currently trading will be lost, and the landing zone is dropped too
[*]Heavy UI mods that rework the trade dialog may cause the ship list to misalign
[/list]

[h1]Why bother[/h1]

Vanilla orbital traders are a phone call and a menu. This mod makes ships [b]actually land on your map[/b]. You have to walk a pawn out to them, the cargo is physically on board, and a ship you damage will spill its goods, demand compensation, or turn on you. Trading finally has a physical presence.
